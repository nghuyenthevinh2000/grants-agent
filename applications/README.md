## 🎯 Program Summary

Following recent frontier AI developments—including high-profile departures from major AI frontier labs (e.g., Jacob Coxon leaving Anthropic) and the Hugging Face security/safety incident—there is surging demand from technical practitioners, students, and civic leaders to understand catastrophic AI risk. 

BlueDot Impact is funding local, in-person events designed to help communities make sense of these developments, evaluate catastrophic risks, and identify concrete actions or learning pathways.

### Key Criteria for Winning Proposals:
1. **Passionate & Informed on Catastrophic AI Risk:** Clear focus on severe/existential risks from advanced AI rather than generic tech hype.
2. **Defined Audience:** Specific target community (e.g., software engineers, university STEM students, policy professionals, local civic groups).
3. **Actionable Takeaways:** Participants leave with clear mental models and actionable next steps (e.g., joining an AI safety reading group, applying for BlueDot Impact courses, or collaborating on technical safety research).
4. **Core Format:** 3–5 hour gathering featuring **one high-quality talk** followed by **structured small-group discussion & action planning**.
5. **Title Convention:** All submissions must title their project as:  
   `Events RFE: [Your City/Group]`

## 📝 Submission Checklist

Before submitting on [bluedot.org/grants/rapid](https://bluedot.org/grants/rapid):

- [ ] Select category: **Events & community**.
- [ ] Title begins with: `Events RFE: [your city/group]`.
- [ ] Ensure date falls before or near **October 23, 2026** (or provide rationale if scheduling in early November).
- [ ] Include link to a shared Google Doc or Notion page containing the draft agenda in the *Work Link* field.
- [ ] Itemize budget transparently (venue, food/drinks, printing/materials, organizer stipend).
- [ ] Mention BlueDot connection (e.g., course alumnus, community member, or select *Other* and explain).
