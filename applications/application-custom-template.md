### 5. Proposed Date, Location, and Agenda
```text
Proposed Date: [e.g., Saturday, October 24, 2026]
Proposed Time: [e.g., 14:00 – 18:00 (4 hours)]
(If scheduled after Oct 23, add: "This date was chosen to maximize weekend attendance for working professionals and allow full 2-week promotion").

Location: [Specify venue name and city, e.g., Modern tech coworking space or university lecture hall with AV facilities].

Agenda Breakdown (Format: 3–5 hours with 1 high-quality talk + extensive discussion):
- 00:00 - 00:30 (30m): Check-in, Welcome Refreshments & Informal Icebreaker.
- 00:30 - 00:45 (15m): Opening Remarks — Why we are here: The current AI moment.
- 00:45 - 01:45 (60m): Featured Talk — [Insert Talk Title, e.g., "Making Sense of AI Risk: Recent Lab Departures, Frontier Capabilities, and Catastrophic Risk Vectors"].
  * Recent lab incidents (Hugging Face breach, high-profile lab resignations).
  * Catastrophic risk mechanisms (autonomous replication, cyber vulnerabilities, loss of control).
  * Overview of solutions & technical open problems.
- 01:45 - 02:10 (25m): Moderated Audience Q&A.
- 02:10 - 02:30 (20m): Refreshment & Networking Break.
- 02:30 - 03:30 (60m): Small-Group Facilitated Breakouts (6–8 per table):
  * Discussion Prompt 1: What recent AI developments worry or surprise you most?
  * Discussion Prompt 2: What are the biggest bottlenecks to ensuring advanced AI remains safe?
  * Discussion Prompt 3: What can our specific community do to engage with or solve these challenges?
- 03:30 - 03:50 (20m): Group Debrief & Action Announcements:
  * Introduction to BlueDot Impact courses (AI Safety Fundamentals).
  * Launch of ongoing reading/discussion group.
- 03:50 - 04:00 (10m): Feedback Surveys, Resource Distribution & Wrap-up.
```

### 6. What do you hope participants will understand or do differently?
```text
Expected Outcomes:
1. Conceptual Clarity: Participants move from vague media anxiety to a clear, nuanced understanding of catastrophic AI risks and frontier lab dynamics.
2. Agency & Relevance: Participants understand how their specific background (engineering, math, policy, civic) can contribute to mitigating risks.
3. Concrete Next Steps: Participants take immediate, structured follow-up action:
   - Applying for BlueDot Impact AI Safety Fundamentals courses.
   - Joining a bi-weekly local reading and study group.
   - Initiating or contributing to safety-focused projects.
```
