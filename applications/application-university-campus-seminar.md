100–150 university students and junior researchers across:

### 6. What do you hope participants will understand or do differently?
```text
Key Outcomes:
1. Rigorous Perspective on Frontier AI: Dispel sensationalized media narratives and replace them with technical, evidence-based understandings of catastrophic AI risk and current frontier laboratory dynamics.
2. Awareness of Open Safety Problems: Show students that AI safety is not merely about social ethics, but is fundamentally an unsolved computer science and mathematical discipline with immense research vacancies.
3. Tangible Next Step into the Safety Pipeline: Prevent the event from being a one-off awareness session by channeling attendees directly into:
   - Enrolling in the student-led AI Safety Reading Group.
   - Submitting applications to BlueDot Impact’s global course cohorts.
   - Selecting AI safety, robustness, or interpretability topics for their capstone projects and graduate research.
```
